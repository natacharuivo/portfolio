Suez Environnement + Singapore's National Water Agency (PUB)

Predictive Water Management for Singapore

Creating a predictive decision-support system that helps operators anticipate, prepare for, and manage critical events with confidence.

Client: Suez Environnement + Singapore's National Water Agency (PUB)
Industry: Environmental Software

Team

2 UX/UI Designers, 2 Product Managers, PUB Department Experts, and SUEZ Business and Engineering Experts.

Responsibilities

Built domain expertise through extensive research, identified key challenges and opportunities, and transformed insights into a user-centered dashboard design.

Context

The Marina Barrage Operations Control Room is a restricted security area closed to the general public, where PUB (Singapore's National Water Agency) officers continuously monitor water levels, tides, and operate the crest gates and drainage pumps.

During a storm or water quality incident, PUB operators need to respond quickly to manage water networks in Singapore. I worked with Suez Environnement to design an integrated dashboard that visualizes data effectively and helps operators make better decisions.

Video sourced from PUB's official website

Product Goals

Redesign a complex landscape of disconnected systems into a seamless operational dashboard, aligning people, processes, and technology to support faster and predictive decision-making and improve cross-functional collaboration across the entire service journey.

Research & Service Design

I led a mixed-methods discovery with PUB operators and cross-functional stakeholders, combining contextual inquiry, shadowing, interviews, and facilitated workshops. I mapped how reservoir-level decisions unfold across the service journey, then translated the research into shared operational needs and design opportunities.

Discovery methods

  1. 01Facilitated workshops to map workflows before, during, and after rain events.
  2. 02Interviewed and shadowed PUB operators to understand control-room work.
  3. 03Mapped decision points and handoffs across water quality, flood control, recirculation, and reservoir management.
  4. 04Synthesized behaviors, pain points, and unmet needs into opportunities for a shared operational dashboard.

Research Findings

  1. 01Operators had to continually monitor the situation to know if a problem was going to occur because there was no alert system.
  2. 02Operators didn't trust the data from the current touchpoints, making them use more touchpoints than predicted.
  3. 03Operators needed to manually compile information from different places to make a decision on a course of action.
  4. 04No visualisation of some data, which hindered operators from seeing patterns or issues.

Outcome and Impact

  1. 01Unified system data to forecast network performance.
  2. 02Presented control strategies and enabled remote facility monitoring.
  3. 03Could reduce stormwater discharge by up to 45%.
